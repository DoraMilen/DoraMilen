# Hi, I am Dora Milen

I am an economist and former banker from Belgrade with 25 years of experience in traditional finance, credit risk, and banking operations. 

After spending two and a half decades inside regulated banking systems, I now explore and write about cryptocurrency, digital wallets, self-custody security, DeFi, and Web3 from the everyday user's perspective.

### Topics I Write About

My independent publication focuses on bridging the gap between complex blockchain engineering and practical, safe everyday usage:

- **[Crypto & Self-Custody](https://doramilen.com/category/crypto/):** Practical guides on hardware and software wallets, seed phrase management, and beginner security without technical jargon.
- **[Markets & Economics](https://doramilen.com/category/markets/):** Analyzing digital asset markets, liquidity, stablecoins, and macroeconomic trends through the lens of a former credit and risk professional.
- **[Web3 Ideas & UX](https://doramilen.com/category/ideas/):** Honest evaluations of decentralized finance (DeFi) interfaces, wallet user experience, and what needs to change for mass adoption.
- **[Field Notes](https://doramilen.com/category/notes/):** Short observations, security reminders, and practical tests of everyday crypto tools.

### Core Philosophy

In traditional banking, user errors can often be reversed by calling a support desk. In Web3, poor user interface design directly leads to permanent financial loss. My goal is to help everyday users navigate digital assets safely, critically, and with realistic expectations.
