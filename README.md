# Hi, I'm Dora Milen

I'm an economist (University of Belgrade) and former banker from Serbia. I spent 25 years working in traditional finance, credit risk, payment operations, and AML compliance before shifting my focus to crypto and Web3.

After two and a half decades inside regulated banking systems, I now look at digital assets, wallets, decentralized finance, as well as everyday banking and fintech apps from the regular user's perspective. Most crypto and financial content is written either by developers for other developers, or by marketers trying to sell a token. I write for regular people who just want to understand how their money moves, how to keep it safe, and where the hidden traps are.

### Why I Write About This

In traditional banking, human error is usually reversible. If you click the wrong button, lose your phone, or get tricked by a bad interface, there is a fraud department or a support desk you can call, even though cross-border wires, compliance holds, and card disputes still trap everyday users in red tape. In Web3, bad user experience leads directly to permanent financial loss.

On my blog, I break down the practical side of using crypto in the real world without the technical jargon. I write about what actually happens under the hood with hardware wallets, seed phrases, token approvals, stablecoin blacklisting, exchange AML freezes, and P2P banking risks, alongside practical guides on traditional banking, wire transfers, and multi-currency fintech apps. I also spend a lot of time looking at wallet UX and pointing out why everyday users still struggle to adopt these tools safely.

Whether you're a regular user trying to protect your funds or a builder who wants to understand how non-developers actually experience your product, my goal is to bridge the gap between complex blockchain and banking systems and practical, everyday reality.
